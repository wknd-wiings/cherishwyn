The problem

When seconds matter, love should not have to search

Everything a nurse, a sitter, or a paramedic needs to know about your loved one is somewhere. The trouble is where.

Some of it ison sticky notes on the fridge, half of them old.
Some of it isin a text thread you would have to scroll for an hour.
Some of it isin your head — and only in your head.
And some of itnobody wrote down at all, because nobody asked.

Cherishwyn asks. Then it writes everything down in one calm, ordered binder — the first ten minutes first, because that is where it counts.

Questions, answered plainly

Everything families ask us

What exactly do I get?

A finished care binder, written from your answers and sent to your email as a file you can print and keep. Her medicines, her doctors, her daily rhythms, her story, the emergency pages — all of it written out and in order. Not blank pages. A binder that is done.

How long does it take?

The questions take a little bit of your time — most families answer them in one sitting, and you can stop and come back whenever you need to. Once you finish, your binder arrives by email within 24 to 48 hours.

What if I don’t know some of the answers?

Write what you know and skip what you don’t. A binder with most of her life in it still helps on the hardest day — and you can add the rest in pen as you learn it. Nobody is grading you. This is help, not homework.

Who is Cherishwyn for?

Anyone caring for a loved one at home — a mother, a father, a husband, a wife, a grandparent. If you are the person everybody calls, and the knowledge lives in your head, Cherishwyn was made for your family.

Is my family’s information safe?

The questions never ask for a full account number or a full policy number — only the last four digits, ever. Your binder is built from your answers and delivered to your email. And we do not keep any of your information: once your binder is generated and sent to you, all of your information is deleted from our side. What your family does with it after that is your family’s business.

What is the difference between the $49 and the $79?

The $49 binder is the complete binder for one caregiver. The $79 Family Edition adds card sets for up to three more named caregivers — each with their role, their tasks, the First 10 Minutes, and the red flags — a longer communication log, and a full second copy of the binder made for sharing.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. Cherishwyn is an organizational tool. It holds the information your family gives it, written the way your family gave it — her own medicines, her own doctors, her own thresholds. It does not recommend, diagnose, or advise. Those jobs belong to her doctors and your family.
